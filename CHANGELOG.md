# Changelog

Every Moovy version, newest first. Details and downloads for each one are on its [release page](https://github.com/ori-eivan/moovy/releases).

| Version | Date | Systems | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1.8 | 2026-10-22 | Windows, Mac | **Stats.** A new **Stats** item in the tray opens a screen with your days: a line for the last 7 days, then one row per day with water and moves done out of shown. Click a day to see each card, its time, and whether you did it or closed it. Kept on your computer only, 30 days; Clear my data deletes it. The tray is shorter: Settings, Stats, Set card position. Copy my stats and Check for updates were removed (updates still arrive on their own). First version delivered by the update card. |
| 0.1.7 | 2026-10-22 | Windows, Mac | **Updates.** Once a day Moovy asks GitHub for the newest version number, nothing about you is sent. A new version downloads quietly, its signature is checked, and your character asks Install or Later. On by default, with a switch on the last setup screen and in Settings; off means no network at all. Tray: Check for updates. Older installs are asked once whether to keep it on. Settings window a little taller. |
| 0.1.6 | 2026-10-21 | Windows, Mac | **Free to use, not for sale.** Code moves from MIT to PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, with an extra permission to use the official app anywhere, including at work. No change to the app. |
| 0.1.5 | 2026-10-21 | Windows, Mac | **Your own pace.** Water and moves get separate intervals: Recommended (water 45, move 60), Active (30, 45), or Custom with two sliders. A short screen lock only pauses the count; 15 minutes locked restarts it. |
| 0.1.4 | 2026-10-20 | Windows, Mac | **Set card position** in the tray: the card pops up and you drag it where you like. |
| 0.1.3 | 2026-10-20 | Windows, Mac (beta) | **Mac beta.** One universal file for Apple silicon and Intel, lives in the menu bar. "Start at login" on Mac. |
| 0.1.2 | 2026-10-20 | Windows | **Quick setup** on first launch: pick a buddy, pick a pace. A hello card shows where Moovy pops up. |
| 0.1.1 | 2026-10-19 | Windows | **Corni**, a cornichon pickle, joins as the second character. |
| 0.1.0 | 2026-10-19 | Windows | **First test release.** A card every 30 minutes of active time, water and 30-second movements in turn. Waits during calls, screen sharing and presentations. |
